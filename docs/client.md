# Client (`client/`)

How the app is built: screens, the "Maison" look, motion, i18n, API calls. Read the part you are changing; CLAUDE.md has the rules.

- `client/` (`@closet-ai/client`): Expo SDK 57, Expo Router (`src/app`), TypeScript.
- On 57, not 58, until Expo Go for SDK 58 reaches the App Store (Expo said "within a few weeks" of the October 2026 release); then upgrade with the `expo-upgrade` skill and unpin `test-renderer`.
- Signed-in screens are tabs in `src/app/(tabs)` (JS `Tabs` with a custom bar, see Look): Closet (`index`, where the app opens), Style it (`style`), Inspiration, Profile.
- Profile is the menu (`src/screens/menu`: the user, what is left of the limits, the sections); `Chip` (`src/components/chip.tsx`) is shared by the add-item form and the item screen; Closet is a 2-column grid of the user's items with photos and an "Add item" button opening the `add-item` modal (photo from camera or gallery, name, category and color chips); Want / Can is a stack screen (`src/screens/want-can`, opened from Inspiration's cover and the menu: pick a screenshot, it uploads as a `want` look and polls, then a summary "You have 2 of 5", the owned wardrobe items as a collage (`src/components/look-collage.tsx`: up to 5 cut-outs at fixed spots on a paper sheet with tape and a handwritten caption, `fonts.hand`; items without a cut-out show as a white-bordered print) and a row per item with its crop and the matched wardrobe item, which opens the item screen); the add-item form links to the `add-look` modal (`src/screens/add-look`: pick a mirror selfie, it uploads as a look and polls, the found items show as rows with their crop, drawn on the phone from the local photo by `look-crop.tsx`, ticked to start with; "Add N items" sends the ticked ones); photos in the form and in the list turn a quarter clockwise (`RotateButton`, `rotatePhoto` in `src/lib/shrink-photo.ts`): the turned copy is uploaded in place of the old one, filled-in fields stay, and an unrecognised photo in the list is tagged again; they can also be cropped to the one item (`PhotoCropper`, `src/components/photo-cropper.tsx`: a full-screen frame with corner handles on gesture-handler, cut on the phone by `cropPhoto`): the crop is uploaded and tagged afresh, and in the form only the fields the user changed by hand stay; the add-item form uploads the photo as soon as it is picked and polls its status, then fills in name, category and colors the user hasn't touched yet (and seasons, which the form doesn't ask for) from the tags; next to "Save" the form has "Save and style it", which saves the item and replaces itself with its outfits (same daily limit as the item screen's button; the compose job retries while the new photo is still processing, five tries); before a photo is picked, the gallery takes up to 20 at once, and several go to the `add-items` modal (`src/screens/add-items`, photos passed as JSON in the route params): each is shrunk and uploaded, three at a time, and polled like the form; recognised ones show with their tagged name and category, ticked, the rest say why; tapping a row opens the same name, category and colour fields as the form (`src/components/item-fields.tsx`), the circle ticks it; "Add N items" waits for every photo and creates the ticked ones from what the list shows and, if some fail, keeps those ticked for another try; tapping a card opens the item screen (`src/app/item/[id].tsx` → `src/screens/item`: the photo with a rotate button (saved on the server at once, unsaved field corrections kept), name, category, color and season chips to correct, the details read off the photo, a "similar in your wardrobe" row, save and delete); category chips above the grid filter it on the client (only categories the closet has, hidden when there is just one); "Edit" in the header (or a long press on a card) shows a delete badge on each card, which asks for confirmation; screen readers get "Delete" as an action on the card.
- The add-item, add-items and add-look modals hold unsaved work behind `useConfirmLeave` (`src/lib/use-confirm-leave.ts`, `usePreventRemove` from `expo-router/react-navigation`): a swipe down on the sheet (easy to trigger by pulling a list past its top) or Android back asks first; the screens' own navigations after saving go through the `leave` it returns.
- Motion: Reanimated 4 CSS transitions and layout animations, curves and durations in `src/motion.ts`, press feedback through `src/components/pressable-scale.tsx`, haptics with `expo-haptics`; Jest uses the Reanimated/Worklets mocks from `client/jest-setup.ts`.
- The rest render `ComingSoon`.
- Menu sections that aren't built yet are disabled with a "soon" tag.
- Icons: stroke paths from the design in `src/components/icon.tsx` (`react-native-svg`). i18n: i18next + react-i18next in `src/i18n`, languages en (fallback), he, ru, picked from the device via `expo-localization`; `t()` keys are typed from `en.json`.
- Auth: Better Auth client with the Expo plugin (`src/lib/auth-client.ts`), session cookie in `expo-secure-store`, sign-in / sign-up screens behind `Stack.Protected` in `src/app/_layout.tsx`.
- The third tab is Inspiration (`src/screens/inspiration`): its cover leads to Want / Can, then "Add looks" (up to 20 from the gallery, three uploads at a time) and the saved looks as a two-column moodboard, long press to delete.
- Style it (Стайлинг / סטיילינג, `src/screens/style-it`, decided 2026-10-21: the owner wanted "what do I wear this with?" as a feature of its own with a glamorous name; it replaced Home) has a cover into it for an item from a photo, a row of closet pieces with the mood chips to style one (same daily limit), and the pieces styled before (`GET /api/wear-with`, newest done requests, a deleted item drops out) to open their outfits again.
- For an item from a photo, the `wear-with-pick` modal (`src/screens/wear-with-pick`) uploads it as `wear`, shows the items found to tap one (one goes straight on), and replaces itself with the outfits screen, which then offers "Add to my closet".
- The item screen has "What do I wear this with?" under the photo (with today's count left): it asks the server and opens `wear-with/[id]` (`src/screens/wear-with`), which polls until the outfits are composed and shows each as a flat lay (`src/components/flat-lay.tsx`: pieces placed by category, the caption handwritten), the occasion, the stylist's idea, the item list, "Missing: …" and like / not me / wore-it buttons (optimistic; wore-it counts up).
- Limits: `src/lib/limits.ts` (`useUsage`, `limitText`) and `limitReached` in `src/lib/api-error.ts` (kept apart from `api.ts` so screens' tests can mock the API and still read errors): the Want / Can and selfie screens show what's left this month and say when it comes back; the add-item screens say when the day's photos or the server's daily stop are used up.
- Look: "Maison" (decided 2026-10-21 after the owner looked at Loewe, Prada, Dior and Bottega Veneta; prototypes in `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-maison`, v3 is the one): a white page, near-black ink, hairlines instead of cards, items on a neutral studio grey (`colors.hero`) as in every brand's catalogue, and one bright colour (`colors.highlight`) for big editorial blocks, chips and the "+" only, never under grid items.
- Big titles in light Cormorant Garamond (`fonts.displayLight`), text in Inter Tight, captions in sentence case, no spaced capitals on product names.
- Motion is deliberately slow for anything that appears (`durations.enter` 900 ms, `settle` 1.4 s), quick for touch feedback.
- The tab bar is a frosted capsule floating over the content (`src/components/tab-bar.tsx`, `expo-blur`): thin icons without labels (screen readers get the titles) and "+" in the middle opening the add-item form; tab screens leave `TAB_BAR_CLEARANCE` at the bottom.
- Tabs: Closet, Style it, "+", Inspiration, Profile.
- Shared pieces in `src/components/maison.tsx` (`ScreenTitle`, `SectionLabel`, `Button`, `maison` styles) and `rise` / `STAGGER_STEP` in `src/motion.ts`: build new screens from them.
- Screen covers (`EditorialCover`, `src/components/cover.tsx`) show one of the user's saved Inspiration looks, a different one a day (`pickCover`), with the words on a frosted strip, and fall back to the bright block when none is saved (the owner's wish, 2026-10-21); Style it and Inspiration (whose cover leads to Want / Can) use it.
- The item screen is full bleed under a see-through header: the cut-out on a blurred wash of its own photo, the name in the light serif, the bright "Style it" button.
- Fields are underlined, not boxed; chips and reactions are hairline pills, selected on `colors.highlight`; photos and rows are square with hairlines between them.
- The closet grid has 10 px gaps between tiles, which rise and settle in a slow stagger; filters are pill chips, the selected one on `colors.highlight`, as is the tab bar's "+".
- From 6 items the closet opens with a "New in your closet" block on `colors.highlight` (`src/screens/closet/new-in.tsx`, the one place an item sits on the bright colour); its big title collapses into a frosted bar on scroll (decoration only, hidden from screen readers).
- Loading states use `HangerLoader` (`src/components/hanger-loader.tsx`: a vector hanger swinging on its hook with a bright tag a beat behind, still for reduced motion) instead of the system spinner; spinners inside buttons stay system ones.
- The splash is white with the same hanger (`assets/images/splash-icon.png`).
- Collage pages (cut-outs on paper, handwritten captions) stay for looks, Want / Can, share cards and empty states.
- Colors and font names in `src/theme.ts`, fonts from `@expo-google-fonts` loaded with `useFonts` in `_layout.tsx` (`src/fonts.ts`): Cormorant Garamond + Inter Tight for en and ru, Frank Ruhl Libre + Heebo for he; handwriting for collage captions only: Marck Script for en and ru, Gveret Levin for he, picked once from the start-up language.
- Use `colors` / `fonts` from the theme instead of raw hex values, and a font family per weight instead of `fontWeight`.
- API calls go through `src/lib/api.ts`: it sends the session cookie (`authClient.getCookie()`, native fetch has no cookie jar) and parses responses with the shared Zod schemas.
- Item photos load with `expo-image` and the same cookie in `headers`, through `src/components/item-photo.tsx`: the cut-out whole on the card colour once there is one, the photo filling the frame until then; the closet keeps polling for 20 s after an item is added so the cut-out replaces the photo.
- The global `fetch` in SDK 57 is `expo/fetch`: it rejects React Native's `{ uri, name, type }` FormData parts, so files go in as `new File(uri)` from `expo-file-system`.
- Before upload `src/lib/shrink-photo.ts` scales photos to about 4.2 MP (by area, so screenshots keep their width) and re-encodes them as JPEG at 90% (room to crop), which also converts iPhone HEIC. Client code uses single quotes (`client/.prettierrc`); the root `.prettierrc` uses double quotes for server and shared, so run Prettier from the right package.
- API URL: `EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL`, or in development the LAN host Metro serves from, port 3000.
- Tests: Jest 29 + jest-expo + Testing Library (`pnpm --filter @closet-ai/client test`). `test-renderer` is pinned to ~1.2 because 1.3 needs React 19.3 and SDK 57 ships React 19.2.
