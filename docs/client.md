# Client (`client/`)

How the app is built: screens, the "Maison" look, motion, i18n, API calls. Read the part you are changing; CLAUDE.md has the rules.

- `client/` (`@closet-ai/client`): Expo SDK 58 (React Native 0.88, React 19.3), Expo Router (`src/app`), TypeScript.
- Signed-in screens are tabs in `src/app/(tabs)` (JS `Tabs` with a custom bar, see Look): Closet (`index`, where the app opens), Style it (`style`), Inspiration, Profile.
- Profile is the menu (`src/screens/menu`, The Thread PR 6, 2026-10-28): "Your thread", her name and her style summary (the tagline before the first brief), then a vertical thread drawn down with stops (`thread.tsx`): today (a bead: Style it today, Recreate the look and selfies left), the last piece styled with its cut-out, counts (pieces · saved looks · styled, "30+" past the 30 the list returns), the first piece and when she joined; a stop without data is left out. Then the language, My profile, the built sections only (set in the display face, no icons) and Report a problem; the language is a `Select` (`src/components/select.tsx`: a row with the current choice that opens the options as a sheet from the bottom, generic over the value), Russian first since Belarus is the first market, no Belarusian locale (owner, 2026-10-28); My profile (`src/screens/profile-details`) is a model's comp card (decided 2026-10-29, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-profile`, direction A): her name large, where she lives and the year she was born, then Height · Tops · Bottoms · Shoes in one row between two thread lines, then silhouette (drawn outlines, `body-shapes.tsx`) and year of birth; a tap opens a value in place on the ground colour with why the stylist wants it, and each change is saved on its own when she's done with it (no Save button; opening another value saves the open one first; a refused value stays open as typed); unknown values are dashed ("+", "add"); countries start with Belarus; then Sign out and Delete account; `Chip` (`src/components/chip.tsx`) is shared by the add-item form and the item screen; Closet is the user's items on clothes rails, one per category (`src/screens/closet/rail.tsx`, decided 2026-10-26, prototype in `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-rail`), with an "Add item" button opening the `add-item` modal (photo from camera or gallery, name, category and color chips); Want / Can is a stack screen (`src/screens/want-can`, opened from Inspiration's cover and the menu: pick a screenshot, it uploads as a `want` look and polls, then a summary "You have 2 of 5", the owned wardrobe items as a collage (`src/components/look-collage.tsx`: up to 5 cut-outs at fixed spots on a paper sheet with tape and a handwritten caption, `fonts.hand`; items without a cut-out show as a white-bordered print) and a row per item with its crop and the matched wardrobe item, which opens the item screen); the add-item form links to the `add-look` modal (`src/screens/add-look`: pick a mirror selfie, it uploads as a look and polls, the found items show as rows with their crop, drawn on the phone from the local photo by `look-crop.tsx`, ticked to start with; "Add N items" sends the ticked ones); photos in the form and in the list turn a quarter clockwise (`RotateButton`, `rotatePhoto` in `src/lib/shrink-photo.ts`): the turned copy is uploaded in place of the old one, filled-in fields stay, and an unrecognised photo in the list is tagged again; they can also be cropped to the one item (`PhotoCropper`, `src/components/photo-cropper.tsx`: a full-screen frame with corner handles on gesture-handler, cut on the phone by `cropPhoto`): the crop is uploaded and tagged afresh, and in the form only the fields the user changed by hand stay; the add-item form uploads the photo as soon as it is picked and polls its status, then fills in name, category and colors the user hasn't touched yet (and seasons, which the form doesn't ask for) from the tags; next to "Save" the form has "Save and style it", which saves the item and replaces itself with its outfits (same daily limit as the item screen's button; the compose job retries while the new photo is still processing, five tries); before a photo is picked, the gallery takes up to 20 at once, and several go to the `add-items` modal (`src/screens/add-items`, photos passed as JSON in the route params): each is shrunk and uploaded, three at a time, and polled like the form; recognised ones show with their tagged name and category, ticked, the rest say why; tapping a row opens the same name, category and colour fields as the form (`src/components/item-fields.tsx`), the circle ticks it; "Add N items" waits for every photo and creates the ticked ones from what the list shows and, if some fail, keeps those ticked for another try; tapping a piece opens the item screen (`src/app/item/[id].tsx` → `src/screens/item`: the photo with a rotate button (saved on the server at once, unsaved field corrections kept), name, category, color and season chips to correct, the details read off the photo, a "similar in your wardrobe" row, save and delete); each rail's "See all" opens `closet/[category]` (`src/screens/closet/category.tsx`: that category as a 2-column grid of `ClosetCard`s with the same edit mode); loading, polling and deleting are shared by both screens in `src/screens/closet/use-closet.ts`; "Edit" in the header (or a long press on a piece) shows a delete badge on each piece, which asks for confirmation; screen readers get "Delete" as an action on the piece.
- Style (decided 2026-10-25): `src/components/style-card.tsx` at the top of the closet offers "Great shopping! Update your style?" when `GET /api/style` has `suggest` (5+ items or 3+ saved looks in a day), shows "Updating" while the brief is written (`src/lib/style.ts` checks every 30 s, at most 10 minutes) and then the new summary; "Not now" hides it until tomorrow (SecureStore). Profile shows the summary under her name, at the head of her thread.
- Profile → "Report a problem" opens a letter to `EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL` (hidden while unset; `client/.env.example`) with the app version, system, language and account id (`src/lib/report-problem.ts`).
- Verdict: the outfits screen of an item from a photo opens with "Worth buying?" (`Verdict` in `src/screens/wear-with`: the call large, on powder blue (`colors.ground`) for a yes, the reason, and the piece she already has like it, which opens its item).
- Wishes (`wishes`, from the menu, with the number waiting for an answer): an item-from-a-photo's outfits have "Add to wishes"; the screen lists them on a dashed thread (decided 2026-10-29, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-wishes`, direction A; dashed because they aren't hers yet: `ThreadRow` `dashed`), each a stop with its bare cut-out, name, verdict and the week to think it over as seven beads (one per day gone, from `askAt`) with the days left in the hand face; a due one sits on the ground colour and asks "Still want it?" with "My advice then: “Buy it”" (Yes, a week more / Not anymore / Bought it, which adds it to the closet first); a bought one leaves toward the start, where the closet is, a let-go one drops away.
- Partner pieces: a look with one hangs it on a dashed string in the mobile and shows a labelled row under the look (`PartnerPiece`, "Partner" or "Partner · demo brand" on the cherry bead), which opens `catalog/[id]` (`src/screens/catalog-item`: picture, brand, name, price; "On the brand's site" only when the brand is real, a note for a demo one). The Edit (`the-edit`, from the menu; «Подборка», «הנבחרים»): the catalog in a 2-column grid, closest to the user's closet first, filtered by category on the phone with the closet's `CategoryFilter`. Want / Can shows `PartnerOffers` (a labelled strip of up to 3) under an item the closet doesn't have exactly.
- The add-item, add-items and add-look modals hold unsaved work behind `useConfirmLeave` (`src/lib/use-confirm-leave.ts`, `usePreventRemove` from `expo-router/react-navigation`): a swipe down on the sheet (easy to trigger by pulling a list past its top) or Android back asks first; the screens' own navigations after saving go through the `leave` it returns.
- Add item on the thread (decided 2026-10-31, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-adding`, "one piece", direction "Выбрано"; `src/screens/add-item`): a powder-blue band (the header too) with the photo as a tilted white print, its name handwritten under it, and round Rotate / Crop / Another photo beside it (`photo-band.tsx`; "Another photo" asks camera or gallery); before a photo, an empty dashed print with how to shoot one, the selfie link and Take a photo / Choose from gallery. Below, the fields as stops on one thread (`field-thread.tsx`) that leaves the band and ends at the hanger of the rail picked: the name edited in place (blue while focused), the colours as round swatches (`color-swatches.tsx`, named for screen readers), brand and price folded behind a dashed "+ add" (opened by itself when the photo shows a brand), and "Hanging it on" with the rails' names. While the photo is read a cherry bead runs down the thread; when the tags land it goes and the fields settle with a short fade. "Hang it in the closet" (the bead button) saves, then plays "Hang it up" (`hang-up.tsx`): the print fades, the photo flies to a rail (no cut-out exists yet when it's saved), its hanger appears, it swings once, "On the Tops rail" is written by hand, and the sheet closes after about 3 s or at a tap; with reduced motion it's simply there. "Hang it and style it" still goes straight to the outfits.
- Motion: Reanimated 4 CSS transitions and layout animations, curves and durations in `src/motion.ts`, press feedback through `src/components/pressable-scale.tsx`, haptics with `expo-haptics`; Jest uses the Reanimated/Worklets mocks from `client/jest-setup.ts`.
- The rest render `ComingSoon`.
- Menu sections that aren't built yet are disabled with a "soon" tag.
- Icons: stroke paths from the design in `src/components/icon.tsx` (`react-native-svg`). i18n: i18next + react-i18next in `src/i18n`, languages en (fallback), he, ru, picked from the device via `expo-localization` until one is chosen in Profile → Language (`src/lib/language.ts`: kept in SecureStore, en ↔ ru in place, Hebrew in or out reloads the app with `reloadAppAsync` for direction and fonts; the direction doesn't change in Expo Go); `t()` keys are typed from `en.json`.
- Auth: Better Auth client with the Expo plugin (`src/lib/auth-client.ts`), session cookie in `expo-secure-store`, sign-in / sign-up screens behind `Stack.Protected` in `src/app/_layout.tsx`.
- The third tab is Inspiration (`src/screens/inspiration`, The Thread, decided 2026-10-28, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-inspiration`, direction C): a row to Recreate the look, then a thread from her style (a bead and the brief's summary). Looks saved after the brief's `writtenAt` (all of them before the first brief) hang under "New" on a dashed garland without hearts, with "Teach my stylist" (a new brief through `useStyle`'s update, once a day; `style:wait` says so) and "Add looks" (up to 20 from the gallery, three uploads at a time; uploads show dimmed on the new garland); the ones the brief has read hang under "Taken to heart" on the solid thread, each tied on with a drawn heart. `garland.tsx` lays the looks out alternating sides (60% wide, a little crooked, the date in the hand face on the side the thread doesn't leave from) and reports where each ties on; the screen draws the thread through those points once all are placed. Long press deletes a look.
- Style it (Стайлинг / סטיילינג, `src/screens/style-it`, decided 2026-10-21: the owner wanted "what do I wear this with?" as a feature of its own with a glamorous name; it replaced Home) is a styling diary on one thread since 2026-10-28 (prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-styleit`, direction C): "From my closet" (the whole closet by category in a sheet, `closet-picker.tsx`, with the mood chips; same daily limit) and "+ From a photo" (dashed: not hers yet), then every piece styled (`GET /api/wear-with`, newest first, a deleted item drops out) as a stop (`diary-stop.tsx`: the bare cut-out, its name, the day in the hand face, and up to three of its looks folded small: three of their pieces on paper, a little heart on the top edge of a liked one, rejected ones left out, sent with the list (`previews` on `GET /api/wear-with`, re-read on each visit), so the diary is one request, not one per stop (2026-10-29); each stop reads its request once). A piece from a photo that isn't hers (`loose`, 2026-10-31) hangs on a dashed stretch of the thread with "In your wishes · 3 days to go" under it once it's a wish (counted from when the list was read), and opens `wear-with/[id]` with its verdict and "Add to wishes" / "Add to my closet" instead of the looks alone. A tap opens a stop, which grows its outfits on the thread in place. "Not mine" folds an outfit (`OutfitCard`, on every outfits screen) into a strip on a dashed branch (`ThreadRow` `loose`) with four one-tap reasons (`DISLIKE_REASONS`, read by the brief) and "Bring it back". Since 2026-10-26 ("The Thread") a chosen piece's outfits grow right on the screen instead of opening the outfits screen: the piece on top with a cherry knot, a thread down the start edge (`ThreadRow`, `src/screens/style-it/thread-row.tsx`, drawn in row by row, mirrored in RTL) branching into each outfit full width, so pieces don't shrink; a heart is drawn around the piece once one of its outfits is liked (owner, 2026-10-27). The looks arriving, failing or coming back empty are announced to screen readers on iOS too (`announceForAccessibility`); "Try again" asks about a closet piece afresh and re-polls a piece styled before. Polling and reactions are shared with the outfits screen (`useWearWith`, `OutfitCard` in `src/screens/wear-with`).
- For an item from a photo, the `wear-with-pick` modal (`src/screens/wear-with-pick`) uploads it as `wear`, shows the items found to tap one (one goes straight on), and replaces itself with the outfits screen, which then offers "Add to my closet".
- The item screen has "What do I wear this with?" under the photo (with today's count left): it asks the server and, since 2026-10-27 (owner), pushes `styled/[id]` (`src/screens/styled`): the piece's outfits on Style it's thread (`StyleThread`, `src/screens/style-it/style-thread.tsx`, the same one the tab uses) over the item screen, so back returns to the piece; the piece's name and photo come in the route params, so the thread hangs at once (`src/lib/styled.ts`: `styledParams`, and `readStyledParams` reads them back; a link without them redirects to `wear-with/[id]`). "Save and style it" in Add item goes there too. A piece from a photo (and a wish) still opens `wear-with/[id]` (`src/screens/wear-with`), on a thread like Style it's since 2026-10-29 (prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-photo-outfits`, direction A): dashed while the piece isn't hers (`ThreadRow` `dashed`), solid once it's in her closet; the piece on top as a bare cut-out with the verdict beside it as the stylist's note (the call in the display face, why by hand), the piece she has like it on a short rail with the new one on a dashed hanger (`Hanger` `dashed`) and "≈", "Add to wishes" and "Add to my closet" in a row; it polls until the outfits are composed and shows each hanging as a mobile (`src/components/outfit-mobile.tsx`, since 2026-10-29 on every outfits screen, Style it too, replacing the flat lay: the look on its own little wire, each piece on a string, in order across as worn and at a length by where it's worn, shoulders short, shoes lowest; her pieces on solid strings, a piece from a photo and partner pieces on dashed ones; each settles with one small swing, still with reduced motion; a little heart on the wire once liked; the caption handwritten below; the lengths are counted from the highest piece, so a lone pair of shoes doesn't hang far below an empty wire; pieces never smaller than 64pt, overlapping a little in a look of six; screen readers hear the look's name and its pieces), the occasion, the stylist's idea, the item list, "Missing: …" and like / not me / wore-it buttons (optimistic; wore-it counts up). A liked outfit gets a heart drawn around its pieces with the thread and filled powder blue (`HandDrawn` in `src/components/hand-drawn.tsx`: a hand-drawn heart or loop from a seed, the line drawing itself in by its dash in 1 s, then the fill; fading when taken back; a look liked before shows its heart complete, and nothing is mounted for one never liked; hearts only where the meaning is love, plain loops elsewhere).
- Limits: `src/lib/limits.ts` (`useUsage`, `limitText`) and `limitReached` in `src/lib/api-error.ts` (kept apart from `api.ts` so screens' tests can mock the API and still read errors): the Want / Can and selfie screens show what's left this month and say when it comes back; the add-item screens say when the day's photos or the server's daily stop are used up.
- Design rules (what each sign means, motion, decided screens): `docs/design.md`.
- Look: "Maison" (decided 2026-10-21 after the owner looked at Loewe, Prada, Dior and Bottega Veneta; prototypes in `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-maison`, v3 is the one): a white page, near-black ink, hairlines instead of cards, items on a neutral studio grey (`colors.hero`) as in every brand's catalogue, and since 2026-10-26 "The Thread" colours (prototype in `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-shell`, direction "C+"): the ink is a deep cherry thread (`colors.text`), soft editorial blocks and anything new or being edited sit on powder blue (`colors.ground`, with thread-coloured text; what it may and may not be used for is in `docs/design.md`), and one bright cherry bead (`colors.bead`, white `colors.onBead` on it) marks the small things to act on: selected chips, small labels, a yes, "+", the bright button; never a big block.
- Big titles in light Cormorant Garamond (`fonts.displayLight`), text in Inter Tight, captions in sentence case, no spaced capitals on product names.
- Motion is deliberately slow for anything that appears (`durations.enter` 900 ms, `settle` 1.4 s), quick for touch feedback.
- The tab bar is a thread (`src/components/tab-bar.tsx`, decided 2026-10-26): the same ink line as the closet rails across the bottom, the tabs as words on it (no icons), the thread sagging under the current one; the sag slides to the next tab from the touch, on a spring, dipping a little deeper on the way (the path is drawn once, twice the bar's width with the sag in the middle, and only moved and stretched by transform on the UI thread, since redrawing an SVG path every frame stuttered on the phone; one sag width for all tabs; immediate with reduced motion); "+" in the middle is a cherry bead on the thread opening the add-item form; tab screens leave `TAB_BAR_CLEARANCE` at the bottom.
- Tabs: Closet, Style it, "+", Inspiration, Profile.
- Shared pieces in `src/components/maison.tsx` (`ScreenTitle`, `SectionLabel`, `Button`, `maison` styles) and `rise` / `STAGGER_STEP` in `src/motion.ts`: build new screens from them.
- The item screen is full bleed under a see-through header: the cut-out on a blurred wash of its own photo, the name in the light serif, the cherry "Style it" button.
- Fields are underlined, not boxed; chips and reactions are hairline pills, selected on `colors.bead`; photos and rows are square with hairlines between them.
- The closet is clothes rails (decided 2026-10-26, replacing the grid of tiles, which read as a shop's product listing): per category an ink line across the screen with the cut-outs hanging from it on white, no tiles and no captions (`RailPiece`), from a coat hanger (tops, dresses, outerwear), a clip hanger (bottoms) or a hook (accessories), drawn in `src/components/hanger.tsx`; shoes stand on a floor line; a photo still waiting for its cut-out hangs as a white-bordered print on a clip. Rails rise in a slow stagger and scroll sideways; the hanging pieces swing with the rail (`src/screens/closet/swing.ts`: the rail measures its speed between scroll events, and changes of speed pull each piece, a damped pendulum stepped every frame on the UI thread; so pieces swing out when the rail starts, turns or stops and hang down again on a steady one; rotation only, and the frame loop runs only while a rail moves or a piece still swings); a deleted piece drops off its rail and the rest slide together; with reduced motion the pieces hang still and a deleted piece only fades; the rails are the categories, so the closet has no filter chips (The Edit keeps them). The selected chip and the tab bar's "+" are on `colors.bead`.
- From 6 items the closet opens with a "New in your closet" block on `colors.ground` (`src/screens/closet/new-in.tsx`; powder blue means something new, see `docs/design.md`); its big title collapses into a frosted bar on scroll (decoration only, hidden from screen readers).
- Loading states use `HangerLoader` (`src/components/hanger-loader.tsx`: a vector hanger swinging on its hook with a cherry tag a beat behind, still for reduced motion) instead of the system spinner; spinners inside buttons stay system ones.
- The splash is white with the same hanger (`assets/images/splash-icon.png`).
- Recreate the look (`src/screens/want-can`, decided 2026-10-31, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-recreate`, direction B with C's empty hangers) replaced its collage with a curtain, "Hers / Yours" (`curtain.tsx`): the screenshot framed around the pieces found (`frameOf`, at most 520 pt high), and over it a curtain dragged sideways by a cherry bead (Gesture Handler pan, sideways only so the screen still scrolls; a haptic on grab). Behind it the same look from her closet, each of her pieces where the look's was on plain paper; a piece she lacks gets an empty dashed hanger (`HANGERS` from `hanger.tsx`) over a faint copy of that part of the screenshot (the photo at the stage's size, clipped by the box, not a zoomed crop per piece) with a partner's price when there is one, but only where there is room (the hanger's width × 72 pt) and it isn't mostly under a piece already hung, largest first (`slotsOf`), so a look of eight overlapping pieces isn't a pile of dashes. With nothing of hers in the look there is no curtain and no beads: the whole screenshot (fitted, not cropped) and "None of it is in your closet yet" over the rows. On opening the curtain draws across to the middle (1.4 s, ease-out; at once with reduced motion). Both sides are placed with `left` / `top` in one frame, since photo pixels aren't mirrored; in Hebrew hers is on the right and the curtain opens the other way. Under it a bead per piece on a thread (full for hers, dashed for the rest; smaller past six) with "You have 3 of 5"; the curtain is hidden from screen readers, and the rows under it (her piece, partner offers) say the same.
- Collage pages (cut-outs on paper, handwritten captions) stay for looks, share cards and empty states.
- Colors and font names in `src/theme.ts`, fonts from `@expo-google-fonts` loaded with `useFonts` in `_layout.tsx` (`src/fonts.ts`): Cormorant Garamond + Inter Tight for en and ru, Frank Ruhl Libre + Heebo for he; handwriting for collage captions only: Marck Script for en and ru, Gveret Levin for he, picked once from the start-up language.
- Use `colors` / `fonts` from the theme instead of raw hex values, and a font family per weight instead of `fontWeight`.
- API calls go through `src/lib/api.ts`: it sends the session cookie (`authClient.getCookie()`, native fetch has no cookie jar) and parses responses with the shared Zod schemas.
- Every cut-out casts a soft shadow that follows its outline, tinted with the thread colour (`cutoutShadow` in `src/components/cutout-shadow.ts`, decided 2026-10-26): `hanging` on the rails, the item screen, New in and item tiles, `flat` in collages and the diary's folded looks; the outfit mobile's pieces hang (`hanging`); on iOS a layer shadow on a view without a background (rasterised on the swinging rails), on Android the `dropShadow` filter (Android 12+, older phones go without), since `elevation` and `boxShadow` are rectangles. Photos without a cut-out keep their print border instead. `ItemPhoto` always holds its image in one wrapper (air and shadow only for a cut-out), so the photo and the arriving cut-out still crossfade.
- Item photos load with `expo-image` and the same cookie in `headers`, through `src/components/item-photo.tsx`: the cut-out whole on the card colour once there is one, the photo filling the frame until then; the closet keeps polling for 20 s after an item is added (only the screen in view) so the cut-out replaces the photo; on the closet rails the cut-out hangs without a tile, and the photo until then is a white-bordered print.
- The global `fetch` (since SDK 57) is `expo/fetch`: it rejects React Native's `{ uri, name, type }` FormData parts, so files go in as `new File(uri)` from `expo-file-system`.
- Before upload `src/lib/shrink-photo.ts` scales photos to about 4.2 MP (by area, so screenshots keep their width) and re-encodes them as JPEG at 90% (room to crop), which also converts iPhone HEIC. Client code uses single quotes (`client/.prettierrc`); the root `.prettierrc` uses double quotes for server and shared, so run Prettier from the right package.
- API URL: `EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL`, or in development the LAN host Metro serves from, port 3000.
- Tests: Jest 30 + jest-expo + Testing Library (`pnpm --filter @closet-ai/client test`). `test-renderer` stays on the version jest-expo pins (1.3.0), so there is one copy. `jest-setup.ts` stubs Reanimated's native initializer: 4.7.1 throws under Jest otherwise.
