# Design rules ("The Thread")

The rules every screen follows, and the screens decided but not built yet. `docs/client.md` says how the built screens work; this file says why they look the way they do and what a new screen must keep. Prototypes live in `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/`. Use the `fashion-creative-director` skill for anything significant.

## The signs and what they mean

Each sign means one thing everywhere. A sign used for decoration stops meaning anything.

- **The thread** (`colors.text`, the ink line) always connects two things: the tab bar, a closet rail, a piece and its outfits, her style and her looks, a piece and the rail it will hang on. A thread that only frames a form or a list is decoration: don't draw it.
- **Solid vs dashed.** Solid is hers. Dashed is not hers yet, not known yet, or optional: a piece from a photo, a wish, a partner piece, an empty hanger, an unknown profile value, an optional field (brand and price), a photo that couldn't be read. A thread turns solid when the piece comes into her closet.
- **The cherry bead** (`colors.bead`, `colors.onBead` on it) marks the small things to act on: the bright button, a selected chip, "+", a yes, a stop on the thread. Never a big block.
- **Powder blue** (`colors.ground`, thread-coloured text on it) means something new arriving or being edited right now (the first screen a new person sees, a new piece being added, a value open for editing in My profile, "New in your closet"), and it is the ground of the soft editorial blocks she should stop and read (a stylist's verdict, the brief's summary). It is never the header or the whole background of an ordinary screen, and never decoration (decided 2026-10-31).
- **The hand-drawn heart** only where love means something: a liked outfit, a look taken to heart, the piece one of whose outfits she liked. Everything else gets a plain loop or nothing (decided 2026-10-26).
- **Hangers and rails** are the closet. Anything that enters the closet hangs on a rail; anything she lacks is an empty dashed hanger.
- **Cut-outs vs prints.** A piece in her closet is a bare cut-out with a soft shadow tinted with the thread colour (`cutoutShadow`, never grey, never a card). A piece not in the closet yet (being added, waiting for its cut-out) is a white-bordered print. Only the hero print is tilted (about 3°); prints in a list tilt ±1° at most, or not at all (target from the prototypes, not in the code yet).
- **Handwriting** (`fonts.hand`: Marck Script for en and ru, Gveret Levin for he) is for short captions only: a date, a note, a label on a photo, the caption after "Hang it up". Never for anything she must read to act.

## Motion

- Things appear slowly, one after another: today `rise` in `src/motion.ts` (opacity and a 24 px rise over `durations.enter`, 900 ms, ease-out), staggered by `STAGGER_STEP` (110 ms). Touch feedback is quick.
- The prototypes since 2026-10-26 use a softer "bloom" (opacity, a 14 px rise and 0.98 scale over `durations.settle`, 1.4 s). It is the target, not built: when it is adopted, change `rise` in one place rather than adding a second entrance.
- Threads draw themselves in (about 2 s), then a bead may run once along them.
- **"Hang it up"** (built in `src/screens/add-item/hang-up.tsx`, for one piece and for the pieces of a selfie; the batch screen not yet) is the signature transition for anything entering the closet (decided 2026-10-31): the print sheds its white frame, the bare cut-out flies to its rail, its hanger appears, and it swings once and settles. In a batch the pieces go one after another (about 140 ms apart). Then a handwritten caption names the rail ("On the Tops rail") or the count ("6 pieces in your closet").
- **Waiting** (decided 2026-10-31, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-loading`): a screen that waits is the same screen in its own layout, never a separate loading layout. Everything already known is on it (title, photo, rail, fields, the thread); one sign means "waiting" app-wide: a small cherry tag (the one from `HangerLoader`; on trial since 2026-11-04 in place of a running bead, which already meant "act on this": the prototype `design-loading/signs.html`, drawn only in `components/waiting-sign.tsx`) running along that screen's own thread, hanging down and swinging as it slows, like the pieces on a rail; on a thread running down it hangs from a short loop to the side (the closet's rail; the add-item fields' thread; in Recreate the look, the curtain's line at the far edge where it will open from, and the beads' thread; the piece's thread on an outfits screen). When the result comes, the tag goes and each part settles into its place with a short fade; the screen does not enter again. Waiting is never drawn dashed: the running tag is the only thing that moves by itself, and everything dashed stands still and means "not hers". So a piece from a photo may hang on a dashed hanger while its looks are composed (it isn't hers), but the dashes never stand for the wait. The wait for the looks on the outfits screen also shows her closet rolling past under the piece, which for a piece from a photo hangs alone on a rail above it as in the prototype's "Собираю" (`ClosetBelt`; owner, 2026-11-09, prototype `design-wear-with-upload`): the waiting is what's really going on, with her own pieces. There the belt replaces the tag (owner, 2026-11-10: "бирку можно не ставить, пусть едет только рейл с нашей одеждой"): no tag on the belt's rail or on the thread, her pieces going past are the wait, and the step words under them say what's happening. With fewer than 5 pieces the belt stands still, and with reduced motion it doesn't roll; the words still say it waits. `HangerLoader` stays for the splash and small waits inside buttons. Built so far: adding a piece, auth, the selfie, the closet and a category (whose rail line under the title stays once the grid has come), the outfits for a piece, Style it, Inspiration, Wishes, the photo for "what do I wear this with?", the item screen (the piece's place, and a rail under it that stays) and My profile (her name and the top line of her measurements). Waiting doesn't draw a value as unknown either: nothing dashed until it's been read. The others still draw `HangerLoader` until their redesigns: The Edit and a piece of The Edit (brand catalog, closed for now). The batch of photos waits on its thread since 2026-11-11.
- Re-rendering a screen in place (a tick, an opened row) never replays its entrance.
- **Cutting a piece** (owner, 2026-11-11, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-strike`, direction A): a tap on one of her pieces in a look (not the piece styled) cuts its string: a short end stays on the wire, a dashed stub runs down (dashed = not hers here), the piece drops about 22 pt and fades, and a handwritten line says "without «Loafers» — I'll remember". A second tap ties it back. Not on a look set aside as "Not mine". Later (owner): C, offering her own pieces of that kind in its place.
- **"Not mine"** (owner, 2026-11-03): the look stays in place, dimmed, and the reasons and "Bring it back" replace the buttons.
- Reduced motion: no flight, no swing, no drawing; things are simply there. The sign of waiting stays too, still at the start of its thread, so she can see the screen is waiting.

## Photos with marks on them

When the app marks things on a photo (pieces found on a selfie, on a look):

- Loops are drawn in two strokes, white and thread, so they hold on light and dark photos.
- Labels are placed largest piece first, each in the first free spot around its piece (above, beside, below, inside), never over another label, and tied to its piece by a short thread.
- A piece too small for a loop (sunglasses, a watch) gets a bead instead.
- A piece left out is shown by its loop going dashed and its label struck through.
- Screen readers get a plain list with checkboxes; the marks are hidden from them.
- Overlapping pieces are normal (a bag over a shirt over a vest): never draw one mark per piece blindly (see `slotsOf` in `src/screens/want-can/curtain.tsx`, PR #99).

## Words

- Russian addresses her as «ты». Copy is short and human: «Твой шкаф ждёт», not "Sign in to continue".
- Design for long Russian strings and for Hebrew (RTL, its own fonts). Photo pixels are never mirrored; layouts around them are.

## App icon

Decided 2026-11-11 (owner, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-icon`, direction A): one coat hanger drawn in the thread colour on powder blue, a cherry bead on the tip of its hook. The same hanger as the rails, sign-up and the empty closet, so tapping the icon lands in the same picture. A sign, not letters: it doesn't depend on the name. Changed 2026-11-12 (owner): no bead (it looked odd on the hook), and shine: the blue lit from the top corner, the hanger glossy wine with a thin white highlight along it and a soft blue shadow; in dark mode the hanger is pink on near-black, since the wine was lost there. Drawn from one SVG, rendered with sharp. iOS has light, dark (its own near-black background, the hanger pink) and tinted (grey) versions; Android an adaptive icon on the same blue with a monochrome layer; the splash is the hanger on blue. Rejected: B, the tab bar's sagging thread with a "+" bead (says nothing about clothes without the name), and C, a cherry swing tag (tied to the waiting sign still on trial, and reads as a price tag).

## The look of the day

Decided 2026-11-12 (owner chose C with A inside, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-today`): on top of Style it, her week as seven beads on a thread (worn days solid, today the cherry bead, days ahead dashed, the high under each), and today's look under it on powder blue, the weather in words (no weather icons: our voice is a newspaper's first line), until she says she wore it: then the blue goes and the line by hand says "worn — I'll remember". No heart on "worn": hearts are for love.

## Смотри, что нашлось (look what turned up)

Decided 2026-11-12 (owner chose D with E inside, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-found`, approved as it is with the slow flip): once a week, on opening the closet, the rail of a piece she hasn't touched flips through its hangers by itself (2.4 s, from about seven hangers before it, the pieces leaning from the motion as on a finger's scroll) and stops with the piece in the middle; it swings, and under it a short thread leads to a handwritten «смотри, что нашлось» and a pill «С чем носить →» (the arrow nudges, mirrored in Hebrew). No blue on the rail. The pill opens «новая жизнь этой вещи» (no declension of her piece's name: the name stands under it on its own): powder blue only as its header band, the piece, and three looks she hasn't worn hanging from it on threads, each with a pill «Надену» that makes it tomorrow's look of the day and turns into «надену завтра ✓»; tomorrow's morning note then brings the piece up by name. A finger on the rail before it arrives takes it, and the line just comes; Reduce Motion: no flip, the rail stands on the piece and the line comes. Only the first two rails flip (further down she may not be looking): there the line just comes. No day counts, no guilt: a find. Rejected first: A (a faded piece with "42 days"), B (piece of the week with a big "42") and C (folded into the look of the day): the number read as a reproach.

## The week's recap

Decided 2026-11-12 (owner chose A, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-hooks`): on Sunday from 17:00, above the look of the day on Style it, the week's thread again, with the look she said she wore hanging under each day's bead (one long string, one short, in turn) and a dashed bead for a day without; above it by hand how many days, and "and not one look twice" when that's true. Nothing for a week she marked nothing in. The closet map (C, worn pieces bright, the rest faint) is for a monthly recap later; sharing the week as a 9:16 picture (B) comes in its own PR.

## Launch

Decided 2026-11-12 (owner chose B, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-launch`): the icon becomes the closet. Over the system launch screen the app draws an exact copy (`LaunchCurtain`): the glossy hanger on powder blue swings once while the app loads; when it's ready the hanger fades, its bottom bar stretches into a thread across the screen and settles on the first rail, the blue melts, and the pieces hang in on that rail. It adds about 0.7 s at most after the app is ready. A slow start shows the bead running on a rail under the hanger (the waiting sign). Reduce Motion: a fade. Signed out, or a first rail off screen: the thread melts with the blue. Rejected: A, the blue lifting like a curtain (a common move; only the swing is ours), and C, a handwritten greeting tag (text to read on every launch; the greeting belongs to the look of the day).

## See all: rails by colour

Decided 2026-11-12 (owner chose C with B's gesture, prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-see-all`): "See all" keeps the pieces on rails, one per colour, the colour she has most of first, named by hand with a swatch. On top, on powder blue (an editorial block), one handwritten line on what her colours say: the one or two she has most of, and the basics (black, white, beige, grey) she has none of. Moving a rail with a finger gives a light tick per hanger, like flipping through hangers in a shop. Rejected: A, tiers of rails three to a row (the grid again, on hangers), and B on its own, one rail with the front piece large (one piece at a time is slow in a big category).

## Empty screens

A screen with nothing of hers yet shows the shape of what will be there, never a line of text in the middle of white (decided 2026-11-11, owner; prototype `~/Desktop/closet/wrinkle-lab/design-empty`).

- **The closet** (direction A, built): the sign-up rail carried on, one rail per kind of piece (Tops, Bottoms, Shoes on a floor), its hangers swaying with faint grey silhouettes on them (`SilhouetteHanger`), "waiting for its first piece" by hand, all on powder blue: it is the first screen a new person sees. A faint silhouette means the same as a dashed hanger, "not hers yet"; the hangers stay solid as on sign-up. A tap on a shape adds a piece with that rail already picked; "Photograph a piece" (the bead) and "Several photos from the gallery" under the band. The blue goes with the first piece.
- **Style it** (built): one example made in advance from the demo catalog (STUDIO EDIT, `server/seed/catalog`), shipped with the app, no AI and no network: a beige blazer as a tilted print with "example" by hand, a thread down to its three looks hanging as mobiles (`OutfitMobile`, on dashed strings: not hers), scrolling sideways. It stands where the diary will be, until her first styling.
- **Inspiration** (built): Inspiration learns her taste, it doesn't recreate the look, so the example shows a look being read: a saved look (the owner's own photo) tied to the garland with a heart, as a learned look is, three handwritten notes tied by threads to what they point at ("the hat does the talking", "a big blazer over a mini", "chunky boots with something light"), above "Your style" as it is today (in the prototype a dashed card said "From notes like these your stylist writes your style"; the app keeps its own line about when the style gets written). It goes with her first saved look.

- **Wishes** (built, owner 2026-11-11, direction A "an example of the week"): a trench from the demo catalog on the diary's dashed thread as a tilted print marked "example", "Think it over", the week's seven beads with three gone and "4 days to go", and by hand "In a week I'll ask: «still want it?»". Under it how a wish gets here and "Photograph a piece", which opens "What do I wear this with?" on a photo, the only way a wish is made. It goes with her first wish.
