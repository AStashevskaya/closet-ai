# Closet: privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

Closet ("the app", "we") is a wardrobe app made by [owner's name or company], [country]. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with and how to delete it. Questions: [contact email].

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your name, email address and password. The password is stored only as a hash; we can't read it.

**Your closet.** Photos of your clothes that you add, and what you or the app write about each item: name, category, colours, seasons, and if you add them, brand and price. The app reads details off each photo (for example "linen, relaxed fit") to fill these in.

**Looks you save and screenshots you check.** Screenshots in Inspiration, and photos you upload for "Want / Can", "Style it" or to add items from a selfie.

**Outfits and your reactions.** The outfits the app suggests and what you do with them (like, not for me, wore it). A short summary of your style is written from your closet, saved looks and reactions, so outfits fit your taste.

**Your details, only if you fill them in.** Country, city, year of birth, height, silhouette and clothing sizes (Profile → My details). All optional.

**Usage counts.** How many AI actions you used, to apply the free limits.

**Planned, not yet active:** crash and error reports (to fix bugs), and the days you open the app (to see whether the app is useful a week later). This policy will be updated before they start.

We don't collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app has no ads trackers.

## Why

- To run the app: store your closet, recognise items in photos, remove photo backgrounds, find similar items and put outfits together.
- To apply the free limits and keep the service's costs in check.
- To answer you when you write to us.

We don't sell your data and don't use your photos or details for advertising. Your photos are never shown to other users.

## Who processes it for us

To do the above, parts of your data are sent to these services, which process it on our behalf:

| Service | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [Hosting provider, e.g. Railway], [region] | everything above | runs the app's server and database |
| Cloudflare (R2) | your photos | stores the photos |
| Anthropic (Claude) | item photos and screenshots, item descriptions, your style summary | recognises items, suggests outfits |
| fal.ai | item photos | removes photo backgrounds |

Each processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy. Item recognition for "similar items" runs on our own server. Some of these services are outside your country (for example in the United States).

When brand partners appear in the app, they will see only totals (how many people saw or opened their items), never who you are, your closet or your details.

## How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. Photos you upload but don't save (and screenshots checked in "Want / Can") are deleted automatically after about a day. When you delete your account, your account, closet, photos, saved looks, outfits, details and usage counts are deleted at once.

## Your choices

- **See and correct** your items and details in the app at any time.
- **Delete your account:** Profile → Delete account. It deletes everything listed above and can't be undone.
- **Ask us** for a copy of your data, or anything else about it: [contact email].

## Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children; if you think a child has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
